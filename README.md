# Mercenary Loadout / 雇佣兵战术套装

## 雇佣兵战术套装

> 将原版背包、爱丽丝背带、皮带和军用护具逐件升级，打造自己的战术携行套装。

## 全套能增加什么？

全部升级并穿齐左右护具后，共有 **17 个物品挂载位、13 个独立口袋和 1 个钥匙容器**。口袋容量合计 81，另有容量 10 的钥匙容器；挂载的工具箱和急救箱还保留各自的容器空间。

新增的独立口袋和格子用于**分类存放物资**，方便把工具、医疗用品、弹药等分开收纳与取用。以下数量按全套升级完成计算。

## 六类战术装备

- **战术背包｜7 个挂位 + 3 个口袋**：上盖口袋容量 5，左右侧袋各 15，额外口袋容量合计 35。水壶、急救箱、工具箱、面具、提灯、电子设备和玩偶各有一个独立挂位，可同时挂载。
- **战术背带｜2 个挂位 + 2 个口袋**：爱丽丝背带增加子弹袋和霰弹袋，容量各 5，并提供对应装填加速；左右肩位各挂一把手枪，共可挂两把手枪。
- **战术皮带｜4 个挂位 + 2 个口袋 + 1 个钥匙容器**：左右便利袋容量各 3，钥匙容器容量 10。两个枪位各自支持手枪或兼容长枪，**可同时挂两把长枪**，也可搭配一长一短或两把手枪。另外两个通用挂环支持兼容的刀剑、锤、警棍、螺丝刀、扳手等工具或武器，以及对讲机、无绳电话。
- **战术护臂｜左右合计 2 个口袋**：军用护臂每侧增加一个容量 5 的独立口袋，两侧合计容量 10。
- **战术护腿｜左右合计 2 个挂位 + 4 个口袋**：军用护腿每侧增加两个容量 5 的口袋和一个手枪位；两侧合计容量 20，可挂两把手枪。
- **战术护胫｜左右合计 2 个挂位**：军用护胫每侧增加一个刀鞘，两侧可各挂一把兼容刀具。

## 背包七类挂载

- **水壶挂位**：水瓶、运动水壶、军用水壶、陶瓷水壶、牛仔水壶和烧瓶。
- **急救箱挂位**：普通、专业急救箱，以及露营、军用急救包。
- **工具箱挂位**：普通及各类专业工具箱、木制工具箱。
- **面具挂位**：防毒面具、半面防毒面具、简易防毒面具、NBC 生化面罩、焊工面罩、万圣节面具、曲棍球面具和骨制面具，包含受支持的滤芯与材质变体。
- **提灯挂位**：防风提灯及其材质变体、丙烷提灯和自制电提灯。
- **电子设备挂位**：CD 机、收音机和对讲机。点击快捷槽自动手持并打开原版设备面板，再次点击关闭面板并收起。
- **玩偶挂位**：背包右侧挂载受支持的普通尺寸玩偶，用于外观装饰。

## 工具箱、急救箱挂上就能用

**背包可以同时挂一个工具箱和一个急救箱。**穿戴背包时，两者仍是可用的随身容器，保留原有内容和存取功能。使用其中的工具、医疗用品时，**不必先手动拖回随身物品栏**：从原版支持的操作菜单使用，游戏会按需要自动取用或转移所需物品。也可以直接把箱中的工具拖到兼容的快捷槽。

## 升级与使用

- **安装钥匙扣**需要 3 个受支持的钥匙圈类物品，三个都必须为空；请先取出其中的钥匙，再进行升级。
- 右键受支持的装备，查看升级需要的材料、部件、工具和技能，按需逐项安装。
- 装备在可访问的容器中时，满足条件后开始升级，会先自动取到随身物品栏。
- 穿戴装备后，通过快捷栏管理挂载物品。CD 机、收音机和对讲机可先手持并打开原版设备面板，再次点击关闭面板并收起。
- 固定口袋各自保留独立空间和内容；装备穿在身上、放在地上或放入其他可访问容器时，都可通过容器侧栏存取。
- 升级后的军用护具仍可左右换穿，口袋、挂件及内容随装备保留。

## 沙盒选项

六类装备分别提供“启用雇佣兵战术……”开关，默认全部开启。

关闭某类装备的开关会清除对应升级并解除挂载；固定口袋及其中物品保留为独立容器，升级材料不返还。重新开启后需要重新升级。

支持 Project Zomboid Build 42.20、单人游戏、多人游戏和专用服务器。

## Mercenary Tactical Loadout

> Upgrade vanilla packs, ALICE rigs, belts and military armor piece by piece to build your own tactical loadout.

## What Does a Full Loadout Add?

Fully upgrade and wear the complete set, including both sides of the armor, for **17 item mounts, 13 separate pouches and 1 key container**. The pouches provide 81 total capacity, plus a capacity-10 key container. Mounted toolboxes and first-aid kits retain their own storage as well.

The extra pouches and compartments help **organize supplies by category**, keeping tools, medical supplies and ammunition separate and easy to access. All counts below assume fully upgraded gear.

## Six Gear Components

- **Tactical Pack | 7 mounts + 3 pouches**: a capacity-5 top pouch and two capacity-15 side pouches add 35 capacity. Separate mounts hold a canteen, first-aid kit, toolbox, mask, lantern, electronic device and plush toy at the same time.
- **Tactical Rig | 2 mounts + 2 pouches**: upgrade an ALICE rig with capacity-5 bullet and shell pouches and their corresponding reload bonuses. Each shoulder holds one pistol, for two pistols in total.
- **Tactical Belt | 4 mounts + 2 pouches + 1 key container**: two capacity-3 utility pouches and a capacity-10 key container. Each of the two gun mounts accepts a pistol or compatible long gun: **carry two long guns simultaneously**, one long gun and one pistol, or two pistols. Two additional utility loops accept compatible knives, swords, hammers, nightsticks, screwdrivers, wrenches and other supported tools or weapons, plus walkie-talkies and cordless phones.
- **Tactical Vambraces | 2 pouches across both arms**: each military vambrace gains one capacity-5 pouch, for 10 total capacity.
- **Tactical Thigh Guards | 2 mounts + 4 pouches across both legs**: each military thigh guard gains two capacity-5 pouches and one pistol mount, for 20 total capacity and two pistols.
- **Tactical Greaves | 2 mounts across both legs**: each military greave gains a sheath for one compatible knife.

## Seven Pack Attachment Categories

- **Canteen mount**: water bottles, sports bottles, military canteens, clay canteens, cowboy canteens and flasks.
- **First-aid mount**: standard and professional first-aid kits, plus camping and military medical bags.
- **Toolbox mount**: standard, specialist and wooden toolboxes.
- **Mask mount**: gas masks, half-face respirators, improvised gas masks, NBC masks, welding masks, Halloween masks, hockey masks and bone masks, including supported filter and material variants.
- **Lantern mount**: hurricane lanterns and their material variants, propane lanterns and crafted electric lanterns.
- **Electronic-device mount**: CD players, radios and walkie-talkies. Click the hotbar slot to hold the device and open its native panel; click again to close the panel and stow it.
- **Plush-toy mount**: supported regular-size plush toys attach to the right side of the pack as decorations.

## Use Supplies Directly from Mounted Kits

**Mount one toolbox and one first-aid kit on the pack at the same time.** While wearing the pack, both remain usable carried containers with their contents and normal storage access intact. **No need to manually drag tools or medical supplies into your main inventory first**: use supported vanilla action menus, and the game retrieves or transfers the required items as needed. You can also drag tools straight from a mounted box into compatible hotbar slots.

## Upgrading and Use

- **Installing the key container** requires 3 supported key-ring items. All three must be empty; remove any keys before starting the upgrade.
- Right-click supported gear to see the materials, components, tools and skills required, then install the upgrades you want.
- If the gear is in an accessible container, starting an available upgrade automatically retrieves it into your inventory first.
- Wear the gear and manage attachments through the hotbar. CD players, radios and walkie-talkies can be held to open their native device panel; click again to close the panel and stow the device.
- Fixed pouches keep separate storage and contents. Access them through the container sidebar while the gear is worn, on the ground or inside another accessible container.
- Upgraded military armor can switch between left and right sides while retaining its pouches, attachments and contents.

## Sandbox Options

Each of the six components has an Enable Mercenary Tactical option. All are enabled by default.

Disabling a component removes its upgrades and detaches mounted items. Fixed pouches and their contents remain as independent containers; upgrade materials are not refunded. Re-enabling a component requires upgrading it again.
