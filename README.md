# Mercenary Loadout / 雇佣兵战术套装

## 雇佣兵战术套装

> 将原版背包、爱丽丝背带、皮带和军用护具逐件升级，打造自己的战术携行套装。

## 六类战术装备

- **战术背包**：增加容量 5 的上盖口袋、左右各容量 15 的侧袋，以及水壶、急救箱、工具箱、面具、提灯、电子设备和玩偶挂位。
- **战术背带**：增加子弹与霰弹口袋，容量各 5，并提供对应装填加速；左右肩位各可安装一把手枪。
- **战术皮带**：增加左右各容量 3 的便利袋、容量 10 的钥匙扣、双枪套和两个手机/工具通用挂环；双枪套支持手枪及兼容的长枪。
- **战术护臂**：原版军用护臂每侧可增加一个容量 5 的口袋。
- **战术护腿**：原版军用护腿每侧可增加两个容量 5 的口袋和一个手枪挂位。
- **战术护胫**：原版军用护胫每侧可增加一个刀鞘。

## 背包挂载

- **电子设备**：仅支持 CD 机、收音机和对讲机。
- **面具**：支持防毒面具（含 NBC 生化面罩及自制款）、呼吸器、焊工面罩、万圣节面具、曲棍球面具和骨制面具，共 24 个物品类型，包含滤芯和材质变体；不包含面巾、围巾、头套、套头袋及口罩。
- **玩偶**：背包右侧可增加一个玩偶挂扣，支持 Spiffo、Pancho 狗等 12 种普通玩偶；不包含巨型 Spiffo、泰迪熊、人偶、鸭子和青蛙。
- **随身容器**：挂载工具箱和急救箱后仍可直接存取物品，也可将工具拖入兼容的快捷槽。

独立口袋和格子用于分类存放物资，方便把工具、医疗用品、弹药等分开收纳与取用。

## 升级与使用

- 右键受支持的装备，查看升级需要的材料、部件、工具和技能，按需逐项安装。
- 装备在可访问的容器中时，满足条件后开始升级，会先自动取到随身物品栏。
- 穿戴装备后，通过快捷栏管理挂载物品。CD 机、收音机和对讲机可先手持并打开原版设备面板，再次点击关闭面板并收起。
- 固定口袋各自保留独立空间和内容；装备穿在身上、放在地上或放入其他可访问容器时，都可通过容器侧栏存取。
- 升级后的军用护具仍可左右换穿，口袋、挂件及内容随装备保留。

## 沙盒选项

六类装备分别提供“启用雇佣兵战术……”开关，默认全部开启。

关闭某类装备的开关会清除对应升级并解除挂载；固定口袋及其中物品保留为独立容器，升级材料不返还。重新开启后需要重新升级。

## 兼容性

支持 Project Zomboid Build 42.20、单人游戏、多人游戏和专用服务器。

## Mercenary Tactical Loadout

> Upgrade vanilla packs, ALICE rigs, belts and military armor piece by piece to build your own tactical loadout.

## Six Gear Components

- **Tactical Pack**: a capacity-5 top pouch, two capacity-15 side pouches, and mounts for a canteen, first-aid kit, toolbox, mask, lantern, electronic device and plush toy.
- **Tactical Rig**: capacity-5 bullet and shell pouches with their corresponding reload bonuses; each shoulder supports one pistol.
- **Tactical Belt**: two capacity-3 utility pouches, a capacity-10 key ring, a double holster and two phone/tool utility loops. The double holster accepts pistols and compatible long guns.
- **Tactical Vambraces**: each vanilla military vambrace can gain one capacity-5 pouch.
- **Tactical Thigh Guards**: each vanilla military thigh guard can gain two capacity-5 pouches and one pistol mount.
- **Tactical Greaves**: each vanilla military greave can gain one knife sheath.

## Pack Attachments

- **Electronic devices**: CD players, radios and walkie-talkies only.
- **Masks**: gas masks (including NBC and improvised models), respirators, welding masks, Halloween masks, hockey masks and bone masks. Supports 24 item types, including filter and material variants; excludes bandanas, scarves, balaclavas, head sacks, dust masks and surgical masks.
- **Plush toys**: add a plush-toy hook on the right side of the pack. Supports 12 regular toys, including Spiffo and Pancho Dog; excludes Giant Spiffo, teddy bears, dolls, rubber ducks and frogs.
- **Carried containers**: access mounted toolboxes and first-aid kits directly, and drag tools into compatible hotbar slots.

Separate pouches and compartments help organize supplies by category, keeping tools, medical supplies and ammunition easy to find and access.

## Upgrading and Use

- Right-click supported gear to see the materials, components, tools and skills required, then install the upgrades you want.
- If the gear is in an accessible container, starting an available upgrade automatically retrieves it into your inventory first.
- Wear the gear and manage attachments through the hotbar. CD players, radios and walkie-talkies can be held to open their native device panel; click again to close the panel and stow the device.
- Fixed pouches keep separate storage and contents. Access them through the container sidebar while the gear is worn, on the ground or inside another accessible container.
- Upgraded military armor can switch between left and right sides while retaining its pouches, attachments and contents.

## Sandbox Options

Each of the six components has an Enable Mercenary Tactical option. All are enabled by default.

Disabling a component removes its upgrades and detaches mounted items. Fixed pouches and their contents remain as independent containers; upgrade materials are not refunded. Re-enabling a component requires upgrading it again.

Supports Project Zomboid Build 42.20, singleplayer, multiplayer and dedicated servers.
