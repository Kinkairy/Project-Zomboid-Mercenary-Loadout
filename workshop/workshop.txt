[*][b]战术背带[/b]：增加子弹与霰弹口袋，容量各 5，并提供对应装填加速；左右肩位各可安装一把手枪。
[*][b]战术皮带[/b]：增加左右各容量 3 的便利袋、容量 10 的钥匙扣、双枪套和两个手机/工具通用挂环；双枪套支持手枪及兼容的长枪。

[h1]背包挂载[/h1]

[list]
[*][b]电子设备[/b]：仅支持 CD 机、收音机和对讲机。
[*][b]面具[/b]：支持防毒面具（含 NBC 生化面罩及自制款）、呼吸器、焊工面罩、万圣节面具、曲棍球面具和骨制面具，共 24 个物品类型，包含滤芯和材质变体；不包含面巾、围巾、头套、套头袋及口罩。
[*][b]玩偶[/b]：背包右侧可增加一个玩偶挂扣，支持 Spiffo、Pancho 狗等 12 种普通玩偶；不包含巨型 Spiffo、泰迪熊、人偶、鸭子和青蛙。
[*][b]随身容器[/b]：挂载工具箱和急救箱后仍可直接存取物品，也可将工具拖入兼容的快捷槽。

[h1]升级与使用[/h1]

[olist]
[*]右键受支持的装备，查看升级需要的材料、部件、工具和技能，按需逐项安装。

[/olist]

[h1]沙盒选项[/h1]

六类装备分别提供“启用雇佣兵战术……”开关，默认全部开启。

关闭某类装备的开关会清除对应升级并解除挂载；固定口袋及其中物品保留为独立容器，升级材料不返还。重新开启后需要重新升级。

[h1]Mercenary Tactical Loadout[/h1]

[quote]Upgrade vanilla packs, ALICE rigs, belts and military armor piece by piece to build your own tactical loadout.[/quote]

[h1]Six Gear Components[/h1]

[list]
[*][b]Tactical Pack[/b]: a capacity-5 top pouch, two capacity-15 side pouches, and mounts for a canteen, first-aid kit, toolbox, mask, lantern, electronic device and plush toy.
[*][b]Tactical Rig[/b]: capacity-5 bullet and shell pouches with their corresponding reload bonuses; each shoulder supports one pistol.
[*][b]Tactical Belt[/b]: two capacity-3 utility pouches, a capacity-10 key ring, a double holster and two phone/tool utility loops. The double holster accepts pistols and compatible long guns.
[*][b]Tactical Vambraces[/b]: each vanilla military vambrace can gain one capacity-5 pouch.
[*][b]Tactical Thigh Guards[/b]: each vanilla military thigh guard can gain two capacity-5 pouches and one pistol mount.
[*][b]Tactical Greaves[/b]: each vanilla military greave can gain one knife sheath.
[/list]

[h1]Pack Attachments[/h1]

[list]
[*][b]Electronic devices[/b]: CD players, radios and walkie-talkies only.
[*][b]Masks[/b]: gas masks (including NBC and improvised models), respirators, welding masks, Halloween masks, hockey masks and bone masks. Supports 24 item types, including filter and material variants; excludes bandanas, scarves, balaclavas, head sacks, dust masks and surgical masks.
[*][b]Plush toys[/b]: add a plush-toy hook on the right side of the pack. Supports 12 regular toys, including Spiffo and Pancho Dog; excludes Giant Spiffo, teddy bears, dolls, rubber ducks and frogs.
[*][b]Carried containers[/b]: access mounted toolboxes and first-aid kits directly, and drag tools into compatible hotbar slots.
[/list]

[h1]Upgrading and Use[/h1]

[olist]
[*]Right-click supported gear to see the materials, components, tools and skills required, then install the upgrades you want.
[*]If the gear is in an accessible container, starting an available upgrade automatically retrieves it into your inventory first.
[*]Wear the gear and manage attachments through the hotbar. CD players, radios and walkie-talkies can be held to open their native device panel; click again to close the panel and stow the device.
[*]Fixed pouches keep separate storage and contents. Access them through the container sidebar while the gear is worn, on the ground or inside another accessible container.
[*]Upgraded military armor can switch between left and right sides while retaining its pouches, attachments and contents.
[/olist]

[h1]Sandbox Options[/h1]

Each of the six components has an Enable Mercenary Tactical option. All are enabled by default.

Disabling a component removes its upgrades and detaches mounted items. Fixed pouches and their contents remain as independent containers; upgrade materials are not refunded. Re-enabling a component requires upgrading it again.

[h1]Compatibility[/h1]

Supports Project Zomboid Build 42.20, singleplayer, multiplayer and dedicated servers.
